## Variable SCHIZ_TARGET

Picks the nearest enemy when you have no target, or your target is dead or friendly. Blocks call it with the line `=GSE.V["SCHIZ_TARGET"]()`; GSE puts the result there:

```
/targetenemy [noexists][dead][help]
```

Its Lua, as it appears under Variables in `/gse`:
