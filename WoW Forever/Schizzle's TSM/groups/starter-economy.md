# Schizzle's Forever TSM: Mining and Blacksmithing in a young economy

Jon Shaw (Schizzle)'s notes for making gold from Mining and Blacksmithing in WoW Forever's first weeks, when everyone is leveling at once and nobody has price history. Reagents below come from Forever's own recipe data (build 1.60.1.70790).

## What sells first
- **Copper and Tin Ore, Copper and Bronze Bars.** Every new Blacksmith, Engineer and Mining leveler needs them in the first days. Bronze Bar = 1 Copper Bar + 1 Tin Bar, so Tin is usually the scarcer half.
- **Rough and Coarse Stone.** Blacksmiths burn them on sharpening stones, weightstones and grinding stones (Rough Grinding Stone takes 2 Rough Stone; Coarse takes 2 Coarse), and Engineers on blasting powder. Players who smelt everything often vendor the stone, so it's cheap to snipe.
- **Silver and Gold Ore.** Rare spawns. Silver Rod (Silver Bar + 2 Rough Grinding Stone) and Golden Rod (Gold Bar + 2 Coarse Grinding Stone) are what Enchanters need for their runed rods, so the bars sell high while Enchanters level.
- **Iron Ore and Bars.** Iron Bars, and Steel Bars (Iron Bar + Coal from a vendor), feed the long middle stretch of Blacksmithing and Engineering.
- **Gems.** Low gems (Malachite, Tigerseye, Shadowgem, Moss Agate, Lesser Moonstone) go to Engineers, Blacksmiths and Enchanters for leveling recipes. Post a few at a time; demand is thin but steady.

## Sharpening stones and weightstones
Rough, Coarse and Heavy sharpening stones and weightstones (1 Rough, Coarse or Heavy Stone each) are cheap to make and sell to every leveling Warrior, Rogue and Paladin. They're in **Blacksmithing > Sharpening & Weightstones** with SZ Commodity, so TSM posts the whole stack and SZ Craft (restock 1 to 3) keeps a few queued.

## Forever-only items
The imports include items new to Forever (marked **new** in [items.md](items.md)):
- **Heavy Thorium Ore** (smelts 1:1 into Heavy Thorium Bar) and **Pitchblende** (2 Pitchblende + 1 Coal = Azerothium Bar).
- **Torbernite**: used in the Legionite Glaive, the Stealthman 52 and two Forever two-hand weapon enchants (Mighty Spell Power and Mighty Healing Power).
- **Azerothium, Heavy Thorium and Legionite Bars**: the new high-end Blacksmithing and Engineering recipes need them (Azerothium Legplates alone takes 18 Heavy Thorium Bars and a Legionite Bar). Legionite Bar is an Alchemy transmute (Heavy Thorium Bar + Fel Crystal), so the Warrior buys it rather than makes it.
- **Decor**: Lodestone and Sharpening Wheel (each 1 Rough Stone + 1 Copper Bar), Rock Garden (2 Heavy Stone + Iron Ore + Silver Bar), Molten Foundry (5 Thorium Bar + 2 Dense Stone + Truesilver Bar), Anvil and Master Forge. Nobody knows what decor will sell for yet; SZ Gear posts one at a time so you can test the price.

None of these have outside price data. Everything rides on your own Auctionator scans (`AtrValue`) until TSM has Forever data.

## Plans
**Blacksmithing > Plans** holds the 93 tradable plans in Forever's data (the 1.12 plans plus Forever's Earthcrusher/Earthweaver plans and the Anvil and Master Forge blueprints). They post one at a time on SZ Gear. A plan bought from a vendor and resold is a known early profit: check the vendor price against the AH before buying.

## Habits for week one
- **Scan before you post.** Run Auctionator's scan, then post with TSM. With no history, yesterday's price is often wrong.
- **Post short.** SZ Commodity posts for the middle duration (8 h) so stale prices clear quickly; rescan and repost often.
- **Let the minimum work.** `szmin` never posts below vendor + 25%, or below crafting cost + 10% for anything you craft. If TSM says "below min", the market is under your cost: hold or smelt instead.
- **Snipe stone and low ore.** Sniper's `szsnipe` flags anything at 60% of value or less.
- **Don't vendor green Blacksmithing gear early.** Fresh characters buy level 10 to 30 gear in the first weeks; SZ Gear posts it for 24 h at value.
