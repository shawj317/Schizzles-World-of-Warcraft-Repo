WoW Forever opens Wednesday Nov 4 at 6 PM EST (3 PM PST), after maintenance from about 1 PM EST. Raids (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia's Lair) open Dec 9.

Why a manual and not one big import: WeakAuras and TradeSkillMaster have no WoW Forever build yet, so the MoP rotation aura and TSM imports can't be used on day one. Musca Auras, a WeakAuras fork for Forever, runs a new visual helper instead (page 4). The helper addon does everything that can be automated; this sheet covers the rest, in order.

1. Put the addons in the Forever folder

  1. Close WoW and Battle.net.
  2. Open C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\. Copy it somewhere safe first.
  3. Copy in the Forever-ready addons (table below) and the SchizzlesWarriorSetup folder from the repo's WoW Forever folder.
  4. Add Musca Auras 1.14.0 for the visual helper. Its folder is called WeakAuras.
  5. Leave TradeSkillMaster, TSM AppHelper, the original WeakAuras and WagoAppCompanion off on Forever.

2. First login: one click

  1. At the character list, click AddOns. Nothing you rely on should say Out of date.
  2. Log in on the Warrior. Click Yes on Set up Schizzle's Warrior on this character? (other classes get the general UI only: no keybinds, bars or aura pack)
  3. Read the chat lines (one per piece: done or skipped), then click Reload. If the button does nothing, type /reload.

4. The visual helper: Schizzle's Warrior Essentials

A Musca Auras pack that shows what's ready, what's missing and what to press next. It never presses anything for you: it's a visual helper, not a rotation bot. v1.1 lights Heroic Strike or Cleave with a glow and gold NEXT SWING text while queued (clears when the swing fires). Install: Musca Auras 1.14.0, then /schizzle auras > Import > /reload, then /schizzle cdm to turn off Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, which would sit in the same spot.

Forever hides combat data from addons, so the pack reacts to what the game shows: no combat log, no proc prediction, no Deep Wounds tracking. A few spell IDs (Enrage, Flurry, Blood Craze, Last Stand, Sweeping Strikes) are still to be checked in game: if one of those buffs never shows, note it. No Musca Auras? Use the Cooldown Manager instead (Warrior Guide).

6. Gold: a brand-new economy

TSM has no Forever build and no Forever market data, so Auctionator runs the auction house for now. Import the Schizzle's Warrior Leveling shopping list (repo: WoW Forever > Schizzle's Warrior > AddOns > Auctionator) in the Shopping tab > Import, and scan once a day.

When TSM updates, make these custom sources (they never use DBMarket):

SourceFormula
szfloormax(VendorSell * 1.25, 1c)
szseenfirst(AtrValue, DBMinBuyout, DBRegionMarketAvg)
szvaluefirst(szseen, Crafting, VendorSell * 4)
szmatfirst(AtrValue, DBMinBuyout, VendorBuy, VendorSell * 4)
szcraft / szminfirst(Crafting, szvalue) / max(szfloor, szcraft * 1.1)
szmax / szsnipemax(szvalue * 2, szfloor * 3) / max(szvalue * 0.6, szfloor)
  • First week: sell cloth, copper, herbs, light leather and bags while everyone levels.
  • Post 2 h or 8 h while prices move; rescan before posting.
  • Copper prices are allowed: undercut by 1c.
  • No Pet Cages on Forever: only companions that are still items can be sold.
  • The full group tree (all professions, dungeon BoEs by bracket, raid BoEs from Dec 9, companions) and operations are in the repo: WoW Forever > Schizzle's TSM.

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
No setup promptType /schizzle all. With the private local file, only the named character is asked.
A piece says skippedThat addon isn't installed or enabled. Install it, then run its command.
A console variable says skippedForever doesn't have that setting; nothing to do.
Reload button does nothingType /reload.
Keys don't workEsc > Key Bindings: untick Character Specific Keybindings, then /schizzle binds.
Bar 1 empty after a stance swapNormal: drag abilities onto bar 1 once in each stance.
Tray missing/schizzle tray. The XP bar hides at 60; the rep bar needs Show as Experience Bar.
Red errorsClick the BugSack minimap icon; note the first error and the addon named in it.
/schizzle auras says Musca isn't loadedInstall Musca Auras 1.14.0 (folder WeakAuras) and turn off any other WeakAuras copy.
Pack icons missing after an updateRun /schizzle auras again and import; it updates the same auras.
An addon "Out of date"Don't tick Load out of date AddOns; update it from its Forever file.
